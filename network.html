    Cheapest mechanistic routes between two genes. Strong, curated and confirmed edges are preferred; edges you rejected are never used.

    How to use this map

    / or the search box finds a gene by symbol, alias or protein name.
    Click a gene to focus its connections; click an edge to read it, check it against live PubMed and rate it.
    ⇢ traces the best routes between two genes. ◎ walks unrated edges one by one (N skips).
    Drag nodes; double-click to unpin. Scroll zooms; drag the background to pan. Esc clears.
    Links are shareable: ?gene=SNCA, ?edge=GBA~SNCA, ?path=GBA~MAPT.

    Suggest a connection — Claude searches
